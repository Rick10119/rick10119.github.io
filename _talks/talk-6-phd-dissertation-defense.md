## Dissertation Defense

I defended my Ph.D. dissertation in Electrical Engineering at Tsinghua University on May 24, 2026.

The dissertation, titled "面向市场化互动的工业负荷建模与优化决策方法", studies industrial load modeling and optimization decision-making methods for market-based grid-load interaction. The work focuses on enabling industrial users to participate in power system and electricity market interactions through computationally efficient modeling, data-driven parameter identification, feasible region approximation, and market-oriented optimization.
